Immersive Real Estate — 6-Slide Pitch Deck

Overview
This is a cold-to-warm pitch deck for an introductory meeting with Paradise Developments. Sean Diamond has received a warm introduction and is presenting the next day. The goal is to make Paradise feel the opportunity — not read about it. The deck should do the talking so Sean doesn't have to.

This is not a capabilities deck. It is a strategic provocation followed by a demonstration. Every slide should earn its place by making the viewer lean in, not lean back.


Brand Reference
Visual Identity
Draw inspiration directly from levelsandlines.com/lizjohnson — the existing slide format for prospect-facing presentations. It sets the exact tone.

• Backgrounds: pure black (#000000 or near-black #0D0D0D). No gradients.
• Primary type: white, uppercase, tracking/letter-spacing applied — feels cinematic, not corporate
• Accent colour: warm gold/amber (#FEC780) used sparingly for the single most important phrase per slide
• Atmosphere: like stepping into a space before it exists — premium, spatial, consequential
• What it must never look like: a SaaS deck, a real estate portal slide, a VR company brochure

Slide-by-Slide Direction
SLIDE 1  —  The Nial Collins Quote

Purpose
This is the opening provocation. It sets the stakes — the market is changing and the developers who adapt will win. It is borrowed authority — a respected industry voice saying what Sean would say himself.

Layout
Full-bleed black slide. Centered composition. Nothing else on screen except the quote, attribution, and L+L logo.

Content — Exact Text
"I've survived four real estate recessions. Every time the market comes back, it comes back differently. The agents who kept doing what they always did drifted toward the exit."

KEY CALLOUT (in gold/amber, bold, uppercase):
"THE ONES WHO ADAPTED OWNED THE MARKET."
ATTRIBUTION:
— Nial Collins, COO · Choice Properties

Visual Direction
• No background imagery — pure black only
• Quote renders in white, spaced tracking, Fragment or Styrene A
• "OWNED THE MARKET" in #FEC780 — the only colour on this slide
• Horizontal rule or thin gold line separates the quote body from the punchline
• Choice Properties logo rendered small and white beside the attribution
• L+L logo bottom centre, white, small
SLIDE 2  —  Immersive Real Estate Will Be Essential in the Recovery

Purpose
This slide answers: "what is Immersive Real Estate and why does it matter right now?" It bridges from the quote's provocation to a concrete solution — without a wall of text.

Layout
Full-bleed atmospheric background — ideally a photo or frame from the Illuminarium footage showing the space, the projection, the scale. A person inside helps communicate the physical reality. Text overlays on the image.

Content
HEADLINE (large, white, Fragment):
Immersive Real Estate
SUBHEAD (smaller, gold or white):
Walk through a space before it exists. At full scale. Physically.

What It Is — Body Copy (minimal, 2–3 lines max)
Not VR. Not a rendering. People stand inside the actual dimensions of an unbuilt space — projected at 1:1 scale on the floor and walls. Real props. Real decisions.

Visual Notes
• Use real Illuminarium footage or photography — not a rendering
• The physical, human-scale nature of the experience must be visible
• Overlay text should feel like it's part of the space — not a caption block
SLIDE 3  —  The Two Technologies

Purpose
Educates the client on the two delivery formats available. Keeps it benefit-focused, not spec-heavy. The split creates a natural comparison.

Layout
Split-panel slide — left half dark (Overhead Projection), right half slightly lighter or contrasting. Each panel: technology name at top (large, tracked caps), benefits as short, punchy lines below. Images or video stills fill each panel behind the text.

Content

OVERHEAD PROJECTION
→  Bigger space, higher walls
→  More widely adopted globally
→  Ready to deploy now
→  Proven at the Illuminarium

LED
→  More mobile — goes where you need it
→  True interactivity
→  Modify content in real time
→  Your permanent sales centre solution


Visual Notes
• Left panel: show an overhead projection image — the Illuminarium, floor plan projected, person inside
• Right panel: show an LED volume — use Japan reference imagery or Kanken-style LED showroom visual
• Sean will provide two videos (one overhead projection, one LED) — embed or link within slide if format allows
• Visual language should feel like a comparison between two powerful tools, not a limitation

Layout
Primarily video. The slide is built around the DiamondCorp video. Minimal surrounding copy — let the footage prove the concept.

SLIDE 6  —  How You Apply It

Purpose
The close. Two clear paths for Paradise to engage — rent the existing space for a sales event, or put the technology permanently into their own sales design centre. No pressure. Two genuine options.

Layout
Two-option layout — each option in its own panel with a clear visual separator. Could be a left/right or top/bottom split. Each option: a label number, a short bold headline, 2–3 lines of context. Ends with an implicit CTA (no hard sell — just "Let's talk").

Content

01
RENT OUR SPACE
A single-day or multi-day sales event.
By-appointment private showings.
We handle setup. You show units.
02
IN YOUR SALES CENTRE
Install the technology permanently.
In your design centre or sales suite.
Your brand. Your space. Your edge.

Footer / Close
Bottom of slide or final frame — L+L logo + wordmark, centred. No hard CTA text needed. The two options are the invitation.

Tone & Design Rules
• One idea per slide. If a slide needs explaining, the slide is wrong.
• Let the video do the heavy lifting on slides 4 and 5 — surround it with air, not bullets.
• Text on slides should be felt, not read. Big, tracked, purposeful.
• Gold (#FEC780) is for the single most important phrase on a slide — use it once per slide, maximum.
• The deck should feel like the experience itself: dark, spatial, premium, consequential.
• No clip art, no icons, no stock photography. Real Illuminarium assets or nothing.
• L+L logo is always white. Always small. Always present.
