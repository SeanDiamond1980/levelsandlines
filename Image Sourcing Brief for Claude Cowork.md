# Image Brief: Overhead Projection Photo

**For:** Paradise Developments pitch deck (Levels + Lines), Slide 3 — "The Two Technologies," Overhead Projection panel

**What's needed:**
A real (not rendered/CG) photo or high-quality still showing an overhead/floor projection immersive real estate installation — the kind used by Illuminarium-style venues. Should show:
- A floor plan or interior layout projected at full scale onto a real physical floor and/or walls
- Ideally a person standing inside the projected space, to convey human scale
- A large, warehouse-like or high-ceiling venue (not a small room)

**Tone / look:**
- Dark, moody, premium — not a bright showroom snapshot
- Documentary/behind-the-scenes feel over marketing-polish
- No visible logos or third-party branding if avoidable

**Must avoid:**
- 3D renderings or CGI walkthroughs (the pitch's whole point is "not a rendering")
- Stock-photo-looking real estate interiors
- Watermarks

**Usage:** Full-bleed background behind white text on a right-half panel, dark gradient overlay applied at the bottom third for legibility. Panel is roughly 960×1080px (portrait-ish crop tolerance welcome).

**Licensing:** Needs to be usable in a client pitch deck — properly licensed or rights-cleared, not just any web image.
